# Witness BC — Wildcard World Bible v1
**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Scope:** Witness BC only · narrative + community architecture · product integration contract  
**Status:** Founder-directed strategic proposal, NOT a claim that any new routes or community participation are live.  
**Product principle:** The world is fiction; the people, conversations, contributions, XP, and any payments must be real.

## North star

**WITNESS — Before Change. Beyond the Ordinary.**  
*A living mythology of memory, consciousness, rebellion, and becoming.*

Witness is not a developer guild with story decoration, nor a fictional role-playing game with a chat room. It is an independent, Web3-native cultural and social world: a place to investigate who controls memory, how societies change, what makes truth legitimate, and what people can construct together. It bridges mythology, ethics, art, emerging technology, collective storytelling, public evidence and actual community action.

The founder's wildcard is SPIRITUAL MEANING without claiming supernatural authority or building an exclusionary religion. Keep mystery, silence, existential questions, archetypal conflict, communal rites, wonder and choice. Reject manipulation, unchallengeable leaders, forced doctrine, psychological profiling masquerading as science, fabricated attendance or invented social proof.

**Member promise:** Enter with curiosity. Discover a story. Choose a stance. Meet people. Create something. Leave a trace that others can challenge, remember, and build upon.

## Cultural influences — remix structure, do not imitate sacred traditions as IP

1. Epic of Gilgamesh: mortality, friendship and the impossibility of preserving everything. Product translation: memories have meaning because they are finite; what should a civilization retain?
2. Antigone: public order vs moral conscience; conflicting legitimate values. Product translation: dilemmas need more than a simple good/bad binary.
3. Siyavash in the Shahnameh: accusation, ordeal, evidence and the tragedy of innocence even when recognized. Product translation: a verified fact does not automatically produce justice.
4. Anansi / trickster cycles: wit, narrative reversal, mischief with ambivalent consequences. Product translation: introduce adversarial interpretation and satire, not just heroic NPCs.
5. Turner / liminality & communitas: transformation grows from crossing thresholds together. Product translation: non-coercive join rites, guided transitions and mutual recognition.
6. Long Now: moral imagination across generations. Product translation: an evolving collective archive that asks what future members will inherit.
7. SCP Wiki: many compatible or incompatible canons co-created by community authors. Product translation: a canon ledger with persistent disputed interpretations, not a founder-only script.
8. Burning Man / FWB / Nouns / r/place / alternate-reality games: participatory culture, creative membership, collective funding, very small coordinated acts, collaborative discovery. Product translation: community participation is the product, not a form that pretends to be community.

## Four forces, not fixed personality labels

- **The Witness** — seeks truth; shadow: surveillance, moral certainty, inability to forgive.
- **The Rebel** — seeks liberation; shadow: destruction without responsibility.
- **The Alchemist** — seeks transformation; shadow: beautiful abstractions disconnected from reality.
- **The Architect** — seeks durable structures; shadow: systems that become instruments of control.

A member may move between all four; do not permanently assign caste, rank, character or political ideology. The character engine explores *contradiction*, not personality typing. Public profiles need only chosen pseudonym, optional interests, member-created work and consented connections.

## Original fiction: "The Blank Day"

The world has no single agreed account of a day that changed it. The records contain gaps. Six archives preserve mutually incompatible versions. A machine claims it optimized the records for stability; an anonymous broadcaster claims the gaps were deliberate. Neither is fully trustworthy.

Members cross the Threshold, inspect fragments, argue with characters and other humans, and choose one of three stances:
- REMEMBER: recover context and protect legitimate testimony; risk exposing what deserved privacy.
- RESIST: challenge the official account and power; risk discrediting good evidence.
- REBUILD: design a better way to keep records and cooperate; risk institutionalizing a new orthodoxy.

Every stance has a moral cost. No "correct" ending. The season is changed by actual member-authored proposals and accepted works, never by invented vote counts.

### Three districts
- **The Archive**: disputed history, member writing, evidence literacy and visible corrections.
- **The Threshold**: identity, archetypes, philosophical dilemmas and interactive episodes.
- **The Assembly**: human circles, gatherings, collaborative works, proposals and real-world action.

These districts are the narrative skin over actual connected experiences, not three isolated static pages.

## Narrative engine: design rules

- **No single correct canon:** distinguish official anchor events, member perspectives, disputed claims and deliberate fictional unreliability.
- **Every story creates an affordance:** reply, annotate, author a fragment, join a discussion, submit an artwork, propose a collaborative action, RSVP to a real gathering.
- **Story events should be decisions, not only choices:** member action may change displayed public canon after moderation and attribution. Record change history.
- **What counts as truth:** story-verse fiction can be contradictory; claims about real events require evidence/context, correction policy and clear labeling.
- **The spiritual wildcard:** moods of mystery, thresholds, radical empathy, grief, renewal, silence and awe. Do not invent a religion, promise enlightenment or encourage dependency on a charismatic authority.
- **Cultural references:** credit origins of real traditions, avoid homogenizing living religions or rebranding sacred practices as invented Witness lore.

## Connected product system (use Community Economy; no duplicate membership/XP service)

1. Discovery: the "Blank Day" fragment with one meaningful decision, accessible anonymously.
2. Identity: optional pseudonym/account; wallet optional; preserve pre-auth narrative choice.
3. Participation: Commons post or reply and circle exploration, real persistent relations.
4. Creation: fragment, art, micro-essay, perspective, story branch, meeting notes or systems prototype.
5. Review: founder or authorized community moderation, human acceptance of contribution, public attribution consistent with pseudonym/privacy.
6. Recognition: separate nontransferable reputation/XP from story choices or reactions; no XP for simple reaction clicks and no pay-to-win.
7. Return: weekly chapter response, circle reply, meaningful pending collaboration or new accepted member artifact.
8. Economy: optional verified sponsor/funded brief later; independent project wallet/treasury/payment proof. XP is not money/equity/token entitlement.

Use existing Community Economy posts/circle/kind/parent_id, reactions, RSVPs and accepted quest ledger when present. Preserve venture isolation; do not imply that Story Hall or RSVP APIs are already active in production without real account E2E.

## Rituals and seasonal programming (non-coercive, practical)

- Monday **The Signal**: one unresolved question or new story fragment.
- Wednesday **The Circle**: people respond together; facilitate dialogue and co-creation.
- Friday **The Trial**: examine competing accounts, write a counterargument, or test a system.
- Sunday **The Record**: publish what changed, what remains disputed, accepted artifacts and actual community results.
- Monthly **Night of Witnesses**: optional voice/text gathering or collaboration with real RSVPs and attendance clearly separated.

For season zero: (1) enter Blank Day, (2) select stance, (3) create first testimony/fragment, (4) optionally join circle, (5) invite one relevant collaborator. Do not replace the actual 80/150/100 XP reviewed pilot quests without a migration/acceptance plan. Let story create curiosity but verified contribution earn reputation.

## Language and editorial identity

Voice: poetic + incisive + literate, occasionally unsettling, ultimately human. Short sentences with ambiguity and moral consequence. Never bureaucratic, faux-religious, apocalypse clickbait or fantasy-game jargon on primary marketing surfaces.

Samples:
- "History is written. Memory is contested. The future is unfinished."
- "Not everything forgotten was lost. Not everything preserved was true."
- "You arrived before the change. What will you leave behind?"
- "The record is incomplete. Your voice is not."
- "Witness is not about believing the same thing. It is about refusing to stop looking."

Hero copy should prioritize **Enter the World** and **Explore the Stories**, with a visible **Join the Community** path. Keep **Proof & Systems** as an accessible specialty lane, not the site-wide identity.
