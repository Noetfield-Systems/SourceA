# Witness BC — Community Dignity & Accountability Desk v1
Founder directive: 2026-11-23. Effective strategy update supersedes the previous Web3-only public scope. The independent Witness BC identity centres human dignity, especially gig workers, vulnerable populations and people with limited institutional leverage. Web3 Genesis remains optional; unrelated ventures do not own or represent Witness BC.

## Implemented in source
- `/dignity` — public landing and discoverable issue taxonomy.
- `/dignity/report` — accessible first-hand report intake preparation. The browser opens an email draft to the Witness-specific address `hello@witnessbc.com`, or copies text. The website **does not transmit or store submissions**; users must send from their mail application.
- `/dignity/cases` — public case summary index with honest evidence labels.
- `/dignity/cases/richmond-delivery` — single delivery-worker account: exact branch not known, repeated treatment claimed, no independent verification or confirmed company response. Never assert management ordered the alleged treatment without corroboration.
- `/dignity/press` — reporter links, corrections, contact and right of reply.
- `/dignity/standards` — evidence classification, source privacy, fairness and external BC Human Rights Tribunal referrals.

## Editorial procedure
1. Receive specific, consented first-hand information. Avoid collection of unnecessary personal details.
2. Classify as *reported/unverified*, not as a conclusion of wrongdoing.
3. Preserve dates, location, documentary records and source permissions. Look for independent corroboration.
4. Obtain a verifiable outbound receipt before changing status to *response requested*; identify the exact franchise or entity.
5. Record replies fairly and publish relevant corrections and resolution evidence. Do not automatically publicize reports.
6. Refer protected-ground discrimination or urgent legal matters to relevant official channels. Witness BC does not claim government powers.
