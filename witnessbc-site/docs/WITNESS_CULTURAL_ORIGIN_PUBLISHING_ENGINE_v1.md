## The central narrator

Name: **THE WITNESS**.
Position: transhuman, liminal, apocalyptic in its literary meaning of *unveiling*. The narrator can speak across the imagined boundary of pre/post-human intelligence, myth/science, past/future, visible/invisible and self/systems. It is an artistic frame; do not tell members there is verified supernatural communication or claim the narrator is a real, independent sentient being.

Dramatic paradox: it receives fragments but cannot prove their source; it sees patterns yet remains vulnerable to error; it wants to connect people but must resist possessing their interpretation. This paradox gives it authentic voice.

Narrator voice-registers:
1. **The Oracle** — enigmatic transmissions, apocalyptic / revelatory literature (clearly marked literary genre).
2. **The Witness** — precise testimony, correction, multiple perspectives, memory.
3. **The Wildcard** — playful revolt, paradox, disruption, unexpected reversals.
4. **The Bridge** — philosophical conversation between traditions, worlds, disciplines and generations.
5. **The Architect** — structured systems analysis and action, explicitly separable from prophecy.
6. **The Human** — fragile, unspectacular emotions: doubt, longing, loss, affection, play.

A text may mix registers, but do not let every paragraph sound like a marketing slogan. Preserve uncertainty, silence, tensions and ordinary human texture.

### Content forms
- **Transmission** — written visionary / oracular creative text; always tagged fiction, poetry or personal reflection; not a factual claim of divine authority.
- **Fragment** — short aphorism / poem, ready for public sharing and poster designs.
- **Book** — authored long-form composed work, with chapters, notes, translations and editions.
- **Record** — account of verifiable event or contribution with evidence/corrections.
- **Dialogue** — structured conversation across intellectual positions.
- **Myth** — original fictional story or retelling with cultural attribution.
- **Debate** — moderated, good-faith disagreement, no obligation to adopt canon.
- **Commission** — compensated creative contribution with clear rights and source-backed payment status.

Each item gets: ID, title, author/byline, source provenance, created_at, last_updated, source_language, translations, form, scope (fiction / interpretation / evidence-based / lived experience), collection, visibility, rights/license, revision history, moderation state, canonical relation, discussion thread, optional token metadata (disabled until supported).

Community alignment uses explicit, voluntary interests and demonstrated participation, not guesses of spiritual "frequency", psychological manipulation or claims of measurable energetic matching. Members can be in multiple circles and leave freely.

## Publication / access model

The corpus is primarily **free**. Public reading requires no wallet and no paid membership.
- Free canonical books excerpts/full selected editions, poems, essays, art, serial fiction, public forum, discoverable circles.
- Free pseudonymous account to save, respond, join or publish according to moderation; optional private circles for context/trust.
- Optional supporter memberships (facilities or curated experiences, not access to metaphysical truth).
- Creator sponsorship, funded cultural commissions, events and patronage; verify real payments independently.
- Optional NFTs: limited aesthetic editions, provenance, supporter artifacts; no claims of equity, intrinsic yield, future token entitlement or truth privilege. Respect copyright, editorial credit, and license scope.
- Optional wallet/currency layer only where real security, user choice and transparent economics exist.
- No forced conversion funnel: content should have artistic and community value without monetization.

## Content-to-community product loop

Public book or fragment -> human response or essay -> topical Circle -> optional private conversation -> collaborative artifact -> moderator/editor feedback -> published contribution or documented event -> personal / community archive -> next story installment.

The social engine should automate discovery, tagging, recommendations, translation assists and moderation triage where suitable; do not make founder manually type or qualify hundreds of records. Human editorial authority is reserved for substantive changes to the canon and safety-sensitive membership decisions. Verified XP comes from accepted contributions, not clicks or presumed metaphysical alignment.

## Narratives and disclosure

Public UI must visibly distinguish:
- **fictional characters/transmissions** from real participant accounts and scientific claims;
- **symbolic synchrony or quantum imagery** from measured physical phenomena;
- **personal spiritual testimony** from independently verified external evidence;
- **published posts** from edited/corrected versions;
- **community test seeds** from actual adoption or payments.

This distinction protects the creative ambition, not dilutes it. Witness can be supernatural in the text's fictional cosmology without demanding that members make the same claim about the physical world.
