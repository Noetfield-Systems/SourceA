The thesis

Community first. Crypto native. Contribution before credentials. Verify what people build. Invite the next builder. Govern together.

WITNESS · SEASON ZERO

I

Real people first

A genuine member contributes, returns and has agency. We don't substitute press releases, institutional contacts or fake leaderboard data for community formation.

II

Open experiments

Reproducible public artifacts and actual review create credible governance knowledge. Local demos are labelled as demos.

III

Crypto as utility

Wallet identity, optional attestations and treasury primitives support real work; they are not a forced prerequisite to join or a promise of speculative profits.

IV

Independent venture

Witness is its own community and brand. Other ventures and infrastructure providers do not represent it or speak on its behalf without explicit authority.

V

Growth through participation

Prioritize Web3 channels, developer ecosystems, builder sprints, social sharing, referrals and member retention. No default institutional email acquisition.

VI

Tell the truth

Blueprint is not a live Guild. Test fixtures are not customers. XP is not a token. A checkout click is not payment. An unissued bounty is not earned capital.
