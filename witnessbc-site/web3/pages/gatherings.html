Season Zero programming

Four gathering formats.

Member-driven sessions; no corporate workshops or institutional approval circuit.

VOICE / ONLINE

The Listening Room

Share an idea without pressure to speak. Hosts invite people to respond with curiosity. Written contributions remain welcome to everyone.

SHOW & TELL

The Open Studio

Artists and makers share what they're creating: a zine, poem, drawing, open-source tool or experimental interaction.

COLLECTIVE FICTION

Story Relay Night

Continue one work across members. The author of each segment is credited and chooses whether it can be remixed.

COMMUNITY DECISIONS

The Question Assembly

Debate which new feature, story theme or ritual to try next. An advisory pulse is not misrepresented as a legally binding DAO vote.
