The culture comes first

No collectible
can replace belonging.

Before the first optional mint, the community should establish original works, actual authors, permission to publish, what collectors receive, and whether a creator wants their work onchain at all.

FIRST-ISSUE ACCEPTANCE
  • Original creator and publication rights recorded
  • Actual community event, story or reviewed contribution exists
  • Metadata, media storage, chain and contract are independently verified
  • Wallet and minting stay optional for ordinary membership
  • No profit, equity, royalty or corporate IP ownership claims without explicit real rights
