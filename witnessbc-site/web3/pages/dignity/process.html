THE SEVEN-STAGE OPERATING STREAM

Every status must have evidence behind it.

01

Receive and acknowledge

Store a confidential first-hand report, generate a private receipt, and allow its owner to track the recorded status. A machine receipt is not a human investigation.

02

Screen for safety, urgency and jurisdiction

Check retaliation concerns, personal safety, vulnerabilities, legal time limits and whether another authority is better equipped to respond.

03

Verify facts and the claimed pattern

Identify dates, the exact branch or organization, relevant procedures and independent corroboration. Record missing facts and conflicting accounts.

04

Route to the accountable system

Find the actual franchisee, service owner, platform, regulator or administrative review channel—not a frontline individual chosen as a target.

05

Request an evidence-linked response

Ask specific questions, preserve a dated sent-message receipt and give the relevant organization a fair opportunity to explain its practice.

06

Track correction and verify outcomes

Distinguish an acknowledgement, a promised remedy and an independently confirmed change in procedure. Follow up when dates are missed.

07

Publish an approved public record

Use source citations, minimal personal details, editorial review, right of reply and visible corrections. Never automatically publicize private complaints.

TREATMENT IS FOR EVERYONE

Systems, not scapegoats.

Witness BC focuses on repeated organizational conduct, unclear policies and inadequate supervision. We do not target employees for their sex, identity, sexual orientation, ethnicity or personal circumstances. Claims that management instructed misconduct need independent support.

Repeated incidents at one particular location cannot be generalized to every business in Richmond or the whole organization. A reported pattern is an issue to investigate, not a conclusion in advance.

Evidence classifications

Reported: first-hand claim only. Documents received: relevant records are held but may be disputed. Independently corroborated: key facts supported by more than one independent source. Disputed: material facts contested or conflicting. Corrected/resolved: documented change with factual explanation.

Internal service targets

Urgent: target first manual triage within 1 business day; high: 2; standard: 3. These are operational goals, not guaranteed response times. Actual regulatory filing deadlines are separate and should never be deferred on the assumption that Witness BC is handling them.

PRIVATE RECORDS / PUBLIC FACTS

A clear boundary

Public Stream entries are edited and sourced separately from confidential reports. A submission receipt never means consent to public naming or publication.

Witness BC is an independent community initiative, not a court, legal representative or official human-rights regulator.
