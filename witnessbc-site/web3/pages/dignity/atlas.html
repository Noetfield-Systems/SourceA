WorkSafeBC outlines employer responsibilities, reporting procedures and investigating workplace bullying or harassment. Coverage depends on the actual employment relationship and incident.

DISCRIMINATION / LAW

Protected-ground discrimination

BC Human Rights Tribunal hears cases involving prohibited discrimination in protected areas. Disrespect alone does not prove a legal violation.

PUBLIC AUTHORITIES

Administrative unfairness

The BC Ombudsperson reviews the fairness of public body decisions and processes. Its mandate generally does not cover privately owned restaurants.

CONSUMERS

Regulated consumer disputes

Consumer Protection BC handles areas assigned by legislation. A complaint solely about rude service is not necessarily within its regulatory powers.
