System before personality

We examine procedures, supervision, training and repeat patterns—not a worker's identity, gender, orientation or minority status.

No invented outcomes

A case is not “under investigation,” a company is not “contacted,” and a policy is not “fixed” until a corresponding action has a verifiable record.

Private by default

Reports and identifying details remain in private storage. Publishing a redacted public case requires separate review and must not imply the allegation has been proven.

INTERNAL WORKLOAD TARGETS

Urgent signals are reviewed sooner.

P1

24-hour triage target

Reports that explicitly flag an immediate safety concern or risk of retaliation. Emergency services remain the correct route for immediate danger.

P2

48-hour triage target

Repeated treatment or a potentially significant service-access barrier, requiring a clearer account of organizational practice.

P3

96-hour triage target

Standard community reports with no indicated urgent signal. All timeframes are Witness BC internal targets, not promises of external resolution.

FIRST PUBLIC QUESTION

Why does one location seem different from the rest?

A delivery worker reports repeated disrespectful treatment at one particular McDonald's location in Richmond. The scope is that specific setting, with limited comparisons elsewhere—not all McDonald's restaurants or Richmond residents. The public record remains an unverified witness account.

View the attributed case brief ↗

What would count as an outcome?

A clear written pickup policy, a documented management explanation, staff guidance, evidence of consistent implementation, or a reasoned unresolved-case closure. A social-media post or an unanswered email does not by itself count as a remedy.
