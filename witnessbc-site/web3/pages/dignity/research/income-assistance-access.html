Income and Disability Assistance: Call Wait Times in BC

Externally documented systemic issue. Source-backed briefing, not a Witness BC complaint or investigation.

Official-source research · no Witness outreach claimed

What the published source establishes

The BC Ombudsperson reported on January 13, 2026 that 2022–2024 data averaged roughly 13% of calls answered within ten minutes, against an 80% government service target. This concerns the specific assistance telephone service, not all provincial services.
