PRIVATE REPORT PREPARATION

Make your concern heard.

Prepare an email on your device. This website does not upload or store your account; you must send the resulting message in your own email app.

What makes a useful report?

Specific dates, locations, descriptions of behaviour, documents you are permitted to share, and the resolution you want. Do not include passwords, identity documents or unrelated people's private details.

This is an independent community initiative, not emergency services or a legal tribunal. Reports are not automatically published.
