CASE WBC-COMMUNITY-001 / RICHMOND, BRITISH COLUMBIA

Delivery workers deserve respect.

A delivery worker reports repeated disrespectful treatment during repeated pickups at one particular McDonald's restaurant in Richmond, BC. This account is not independently verified. The specific branch remains unidentified in this public brief. The concern does not characterize all restaurants or all of Richmond.

REPORT · NOT INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED

What the worker reports

The worker reports repeated disrespectful interactions involving different staff at one particular branch and some concerns from limited additional McDonald's visits. It is not a claim about all McDonald's locations in Richmond. The worker is concerned about management practices and supervision rather than any individual employee; the cause remains unverified. This suspicion is not a verified finding about management.

What is not established

The precise branch, dates, relevant written restaurant policies, corroborating evidence, direction from any manager, and company response have not been established. No protected-ground discrimination or unlawful act has been determined.

Questions requiring a response

  • What are McDonald's written procedures for delivery-driver pickups?
  • How are staff trained and supervised to treat delivery partners respectfully?
  • Are local rules consistent with the franchise's official policy?
  • How can drivers complain without retaliation?
  • Will the relevant owner or regional management review this concern?

Verification and right of reply

The next steps are to identify the location, record first-hand incident details, request the relevant operator's response and publish corrections when supported by evidence. No outreach or formal response has been verified for this case yet.

Witnesses and company representatives can provide corrections or a response through the press and right-of-reply desk.
