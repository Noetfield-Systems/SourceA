The Prologue / Before Change

They called it
The Quieting.

A century after the cities learned to predict every desire, nobody remembered who had chosen the rules. Public records still existed, but the lines that mattered changed each night. The great machines did not lie. They simply forgot whenever their owners asked.

At dawn, six strangers received a message written in an alphabet nobody had used for generations: “If the world can be rewritten, who gets to be the witness?”

FOUNDING CONFLICT

The Record is broken.

Memory has become property. Civic decisions are executed by invisible systems. Communities have been divided by predictive borders. Beneath the city, a hidden network keeps fragments of the unaltered past.

  • What is justice when evidence can vanish?
  • What is freedom when choices are predicted?
  • What is a society when nobody trusts shared history?
