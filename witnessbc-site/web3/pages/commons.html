The Witness Prompt

What did you witness
before it changed?

This is the first open invitation. A story may be personal, imagined, historical with sources, or a reflection. Never tell someone else's private story without consent.

Explore the first prompt ↗
HOW A STORY STARTS
  • I noticed: one detail that changed how I saw something
  • I wondered: a question nobody asked yet
  • I made: a short story, artwork or voice transcript
  • I invited: another person to continue the conversation

These are prompts, not fictional member testimonies.
