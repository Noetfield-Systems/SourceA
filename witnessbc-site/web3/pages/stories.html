Story prompt / 001

Before Change.

Think of a moment just before something important changed. It can be the first sentence of an imagined world, a conversation, or a personal memory you freely choose to share.

Start with: “Before it changed, I remember…”

Participation, not extraction

Write what belongs to you.

  • Original fiction, poems, observations and public-source essays are welcome
  • Credit collaborators and distinguish fact from fiction
  • Share only memories you are comfortable publishing
  • Get consent for stories about identifiable people
  • Ask questions; do not pressure others to disclose trauma

EDITORIAL FICTION / 001THE THRESHOLD

The day nobody named

There was a morning when the town noticed its quietest clock had stopped. Nobody agreed when. The baker said yesterday, the archivist said a century ago, and the youngest child asked why everyone needed the same answer.

What happens when the child follows the clock's shadow?

EDITORIAL FICTION / 002THE WITNESS

The room of unheard things

The room was not hidden. It simply opened only when someone arrived without a prepared speech. Inside stood shelves labelled with questions rather than names. One shelf was empty. Its sign read: What will you remember for someone else?

What should the first visitor leave on that shelf?

EDITORIAL FICTION / 003THE BRIDGE

Letters after tomorrow

In a city built on bridges, the last messenger carried a letter addressed to a person who had not been born. The letter contained no instructions, only an invitation: Find what changed, and tell us what you chose to keep.

Who reads the letter first, and why?
